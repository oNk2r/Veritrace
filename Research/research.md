# Veritrace Research

## Project

**Name:** Veritrace

**Tagline:**
Verify sustainability claims with AI.

---

## Problem Statement

Companies publish ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) or sustainability reports that include information about their greenhouse gas emissions.

These reports are primarily self-reported. Independent datasets such as Climate TRACE estimate emissions using satellite observations and other external data.

Currently, comparing these two sources is time-consuming and requires manual analysis.

Veritrace aims to simplify this process by extracting emissions data from ESG reports, comparing it with independent emissions datasets, and generating an explainable discrepancy report using AI.

---

## Why this matters

- Sustainability reporting is becoming increasingly important.
- Investors and regulators rely on ESG disclosures.
- Independent verification improves transparency.
- Manual comparison of reports and external datasets is slow.

---

## Project Goal

Build an AI-powered web application that:

- extracts emissions data from ESG reports
- retrieves independent emissions data
- compares both datasets
- highlights discrepancies
- explains possible reasons using AI

---

## Target Users

- ESG Analysts
- Sustainability Teams
- Investors
- Journalists
- Researchers
- Students

---

## Core Features (MVP)

- Upload ESG report (PDF)
- AI extracts emissions data
- Compare with Climate TRACE
- Generate discrepancy report
- Dashboard with charts

---

## Stretch Goals

- Facility map
- Company search
- Trust score
- AI chat
- Multi-year comparison

---

## Success Criteria

A user uploads an ESG report and receives an understandable report comparing disclosed emissions with independent data in less than one minute.
